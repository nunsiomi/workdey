how it works

Set it up once. It keeps working.

Five steps, and only the first one is yours.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Paste your CV text and add your skills. Pick the roles you want, where you can work, and how often you want to hear from us.

  2. 2

    We scan while you sleep

    WorkDey runs by itself on a schedule. It reads MyJobMag, Jobberman and remote job feeds, drops reposts and scams, and keeps the posts that fit your profile.

    LinkedIn, X and Instagram posts are coming next.
  3. 3

    Get jobs in your inbox

    You get a short email with your best match and why it fits. No feed to scroll, no site to check.

  4. 4

    Review and apply

    Open the email, read the jobs, copy the reply or cover letter we drafted, and open the original post to apply.

  5. 5

    Improve your CV

    Each of your top jobs comes with 3 to 5 changes to make your CV fit it, plus a rewritten summary you can copy.
